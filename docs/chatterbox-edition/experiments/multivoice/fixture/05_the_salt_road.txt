# title: The Salt Road
# characters: Mara Vale=female, Jonah Reed=male, Mrs. Reed=female, Pell=male
# aliases: Mrs. Reed=Mother; Pell=the carter

The wagon stopped where the salt road bent toward the marsh, and the carter climbed down to look at the wheel. Mara stayed on the bench with the reins slack in her hands.

From the back of the wagon came Jonah's voice: «Jonah Reed»"How long are we stopping for?" Pell answered without looking up: «Pell»"Until this wheel stops wobbling, lad."

«Jonah Reed»"That could be all night." Jonah climbed over the sacks and dropped into the mud. «Jonah Reed»"I'll walk."

«Mara Vale»"You'll sink," Mara said. «Mara Vale»"The marsh takes boots."

«Jonah Reed»"Then I'll walk barefoot." «Pell»"Nobody walks," Pell muttered.

Mrs. Reed pulled the canvas aside and squinted into the rain. «Mrs. Reed»"Jonah, get back in here."

«Jonah Reed»"We should," he said, «Jonah Reed»"at least see how far the next inn is."

Mara asked the carter, quietly: «Mara Vale»"Is it the axle?"

«Pell»"It's the pin. Sheared clean through." He held it up. «Pell»"Thirty years, and I've never seen one go like that."

«Mara Vale»"Can you mend it?"

«Pell»"With what? Salt?"

Jonah laughed from the ditch. «Jonah Reed»"There's plenty of that."

«Mrs. Reed»"Don't be clever," Mother snapped. «Mrs. Reed»"Help him."

Mara climbed down beside Pell and turned the broken pin over in her fingers: «Mara Vale»"Brass would hold." Pell grunted: «Pell»"If we had brass."

«Mara Vale»"My mother's locket is brass." She was already unfastening it. «Mara Vale»"Take it."

«Jonah Reed»"Mara, no," Jonah whispered. «Jonah Reed»"That was hers."

«Mara Vale»"She'd have thrown it at the wheel herself."

For a long moment no one spoke. Then Mrs. Reed said, gently: «Mrs. Reed»"She would, at that."

Pell took the locket and weighed it in his palm, and then he was kneeling in the mud with his knife, working the soft metal into the hole where the pin had been.

«Pell»"Hold the lamp closer," he ordered. «Pell»"There. Now hold it still."

«Jonah Reed»"I am holding it still."

«Pell»"You're shaking like a reed, Reed."

Jonah opened his mouth, closed it, and laughed despite himself. Mara answered for him: «Mara Vale»"He's cold. We all are."

«Pell»"Then we'd best get rolling." Pell stood, wiped his hands on his coat, and climbed back to the bench. «Pell»"In, all of you. The inn's an hour off, if the pin holds."

«Mrs. Reed»"And if it doesn't?"

«Pell»"Then you'll walk," Pell said, «Pell»"barefoot, like the lad wanted."
